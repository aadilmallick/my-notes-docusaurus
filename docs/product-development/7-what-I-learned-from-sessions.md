## Week 1

### Fireside chat

#### Networking is important

**The Power of Networking**: The speaker repeatedly stresses that building a long-term network is the most critical resource for an entrepreneur, explaining that it directly facilitates team recruitment, partnerships, funding, and exit opportunities. For early-career founders who may feel they have little to offer, the speaker notes that their greatest asset is their time.

#### Software is no longer a moat

**Modern Startup Moats (Distribution and Data)**: The speaker asserts that software features and visual designs are easily copied overnight in the current AI landscape, meaning software itself is no longer a defensible moat. Instead, the only true remaining advantages are proprietary data and distribution edges.

If your differentiation is a software feature, you are cooked.

The only two moats are 1) distribution and 2) data
