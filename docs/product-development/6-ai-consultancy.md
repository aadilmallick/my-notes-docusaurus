## Opportunities

### Teaching AI to people

One of the best opportunities, if you have experience and expertise in Claude Code and codex, is to try to target other people and teach them about it.

You can target small businesses or go for mid-sized companies and teach teams and charge them, or you could even teach people individually or make a school community about it.

Basically, the possibilities are endless, and the important point is to try to get a funnel. 

Here is how you get a funnel:

1. **real workflow demos**: Post on LinkedIn and other platforms about your workflow. Show the artifacts you built, just to get people interested in what you can do with a lot of coding codex, and showcase your abilities. 
2. **free 45 min live intro**: Post somewhere an entire 45‑minute intro to AI that explains everything a person could possibly need to get started.
