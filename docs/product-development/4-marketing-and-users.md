# How to get users (0 to 1,000+)

### What is Marketing?
- Marketing is not just "promotion"; **marketing is a conversation**.
- You could have the best product in the world, but if no one hears or cares about it, it won't matter.
- *"Best-selling author, not best-writing author"* — distribution and narrative matter as much as (or more than) product quality.
- The secret behind marketing: Figure out **what makes your product special** and **how to make people care about it**.
- If people do not care about your product, no amount of publicity or reach will make them buy it.

#### Core Principles of Messaging
- **Sell the story, not features; sell a lifestyle, not specs.**
  - *Example:* The original iPod ad didn't say "5GB hard drive"; it said *"1,000 songs in your pocket."*
  - Avoid technical jargon (e.g., bragging about Solana, React Native, or specific AI model parameters). Users care about how their lives will change, not the underlying tech stack.

#### Copywriting Framework
1. **Headline:** Grab someone's attention immediately.
2. **Body:** Keep content enticing so they stay until the end.
3. **CTA (Call to Action):** Give a clear, direct step to convert them into a customer or user.

### Channels & Focus

> [!NOTE]
> **Pick ONE channel and master it.** Do not split focus across LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram, TikTok, cold email, etc., simultaneously when starting out.

- Choose **one main channel** based on your team's unfair advantage (e.g., if you write well $\rightarrow$ LinkedIn/Twitter/Reddit; if you speak/present well $\rightarrow$ Video/TikTok/YouTube).
- **Drive relentless volume:** Ask yourself: *"How much volume can I put into this single channel such that it becomes statistically unreasonable for me to fail?"*

#### Channel Breakdown & Strategies:
- **LinkedIn Strategy (B2B / Professional):**
  - Commit to **3 posts a week for 6 months**.
  - **Month 1:** Announce your company/product, build in public, document the journey.
  - **Month 2+:** Shares case studies, early customer wins, and social proof.
  - *Content heuristic:* Post anything that is even bare-minimum newsworthy (getting an office, new feature release, event hosted).
  - *Mindset:* Don't fear looking "cringe" or uncomfortable. Volume builds skill, lowers friction, and generates results.
- **AEO / GEO (AI Engine Optimization):**
  - Optimizing product positioning so LLMs (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude) recommend your product when users ask for tools in your niche.
- **Warm Outreach / "Hand-to-Hand Combat":**
  - Engaging directly with target accounts on LinkedIn or Twitter (commenting, interacting) before sending a direct pitch. Turning cold outreach into warm connections.

### Product Validation & Customer Discovery

> [!NOTE]
> **Stop building. Your problem isn't building—it's fear of getting out of your house and talking to people.**

#### The 3 Steps of Marketing:
1. Talk to people.
2. Genuinely understand their problems (ask, don't pitch).
3. Offer a solution to their specific problem.

#### The "Vitamins vs. Painkillers" Framework:
- **Vitamins:** Nice-to-haves (non-essential improvements).
- **Painkillers:** Solves a pressing, immediate problem people are actively conscious of (e.g., *"If you have a headache, you want a Tylenol, not Vitamin C"*).

### The 3 Phases of Startup Growth

#### Phase 0: Creating the Idea
A valid startup idea must satisfy one of two criteria:
1. Solves a problem you have **personally experienced**.
2. Solves a painful problem for a **very specific demographic you have direct access to**.
- *If you do neither, you are likely building a solution in search of a problem.*

#### Phase 1: 0 to 10 Users (Finding Champions)
- **Do NOT pitch your idea during discovery.** Ask about their day-to-day workflow, pain points, and where they lose time.
- **Niche down as far as possible:** Don't build for "all CRM users." Build for "CFOs of public companies doing SOX compliance" or "Pathologists in Houston, TX."
- **Focus:** Build an app that **10–20 people deeply care about**. Distribution scaling comes later.

#### Phase 2: 10 to 300 Users (Proving Distribution)
- Find where your specific ICP goes to discover new products (not just where they hangout, but where they buy).
- Scale through 1 single distribution channel using organic content, pre-orders, waitlists, or direct outreach.

#### Phase 3: 300 to 1,000+ Users (Scaling Revenue)
- Reaching $1M ARR in Phase 3 is about **doubling down on what worked in Phase 2**:
  - **Scale Horizontally:** Create multiple accounts/channels on the same platform.
  - **Scale Vertically:** Higher volume, higher production quality, paid UGC, or ads.

### Common Mistakes

1. **Pitching instead of asking:** Coming with a 100-feature MVP and asking *"Would you buy this?"* creates false feedback. Ask broad questions about their day-to-day workflow first to uncover latent pain.
2. **Building for demographics you don't know:** Pick a niche close to your existing network or personal experience.
3. **Feature trapping (The "One More Feature" Illusion):** Adding features because a prospect says *"I'll buy it if you add X."* If it's a true painkiller, they'll buy or test early.
4. **Marketplace Trap:** Trying to acquire supply and demand at the same time. **Hack:** Focus on the side of the market that feels the pain most frequently and severely—they will pull the other side in.
5. **Channel Jumping:** Trying 4 social platforms at once without generating enough volume on any single one.
